## Bark textures

`textures/bark/Bark001_*`, `Bark002_*` and `Bark003_*` are from [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com)
(Bark001, Bark002, Bark003), released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). They were downscaled to 512 pixels.
The leaf textures were downscaled to 512 × 512.
