# 0073 — HLOD: proxies for streamed and authored cells

- **Status:** draft (rewritten 2026-11-13 for generated cells). Built in two parts: runtime proxies
  first; offline proxies for file cells once 0072's importer loads them.
- **Packages:** `@aethervtt/shard-hlod` (new), `@aethervtt/shard-render`, `@aethervtt/shard-structure`,
  `@aethervtt/shard-partition`, `@aethervtt/shard-mesh`, `apps/cli`
- **Depends on:** 0022, 0055, 0067, 0072, 0103, 0113, 0116

## Context

0113 streams a town as generated cells: `full` near a player, `far` shells (outer walls, doors,
windows, roofs, ground) out to the last ring. Risen draws 16 full and ~84 far cells, and its frame
is over budget. Measured on an Apple M5 Max (headless Chromium, 1920×1080, CPU-bound, 2026-11-13;
GPU untimed):

| | Mesh slots | Camera draws | CPU ms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whole frame | 18,736 (16,650 distinct batches) | 3,391 | 16.5 (p95 17.2) |
| Full ring houses (16 cells) | 5,053 | 1,075 | 2.9–4.9 |
| Far ring houses (80 cells) | 4,158 | 247 | 2.5–2.6 |
| Ground (structure's global group, 8 m chunks, all cells) | 8,107 | 2,057 | 2.8–4.6 |
| Everything hidden | 18,724 hidden | 12 | 6.5 |

Three things follow:

- **Slots cost, drawn or not.** Hiding everything leaves 6.5 ms of queueing, uploads and instance
  preparation. A proxy must remove its cell's slots, not hide them.
- **Structure has no LOD, and every chunk is its own batch.** Far shells draw at full detail as
  thousands of 8 m chunks per material, the ground most of all.
- **Occlusion doesn't pay yet.** It halves camera draws, but costs more than it saves over 16.6k
  single-instance batches: the frame goes from 16.5 to 18.4 ms.

Baking offline from 0072's cell files can't serve this alone. Cells are generated on each client,
they change while loaded, and what draws is added after commit by game code (Risen's client dresses
cells with meshes and runtime materials, since fragments carry no render handles). So this spec
builds proxies on the client, from what it draws, while it runs. Authored file cells also get
proxies built offline by the same build, cached, and optionally `baked` into one textured mesh.

## Goals

- A proxy per cell of a `proxy` level, built at runtime on the worker pool from the cell's committed,
  dressed entities, under a per-frame main-thread budget, never stalling a frame.
- Structure (walls, openings, frames, door leaves, roofs, floors) in the proxy, with LODs inside it.
- Rebuilt when the cell's delta or dressing changes, and only that proxy. A stale proxy draws until
  its replacement lands.
- One swap distance: a cell ↔ its proxy at the level swap, where only interiors change. Proxy
  windows are dark.
- Exact coverage on every frame: each cell's area draws once, never zero or two times.
- Proxies cast into 0116's static layers and occlude like the cells they replace.
- Near structure merged per 32 m region for camera views, so the full ring costs regions, not chunks.
- Both tiers: correct on the WebGL2 baseline, if slower.
- File cells (0072): proxies built offline by `shard hlod build`, cached by content, loaded instead
  of built at runtime. The `merged` method, or `baked`: one simplified mesh with base colour, normal
  and ORM textures baked from the originals through the G-buffer, for any 0020 material. Two
  builds give identical bytes.

## Non-goals

- Proxies for unloaded cells, past the last ring (see Open questions). Headless runs and servers
  build nothing at runtime.
- `baked` at runtime. See Decisions for the numbers.
- Terrain (0071), scatter (0045), lights and lit windows, characters (0115), crossfades.

## Design

### Levels, coverage and the swap

- A grid level with `proxy: true` (Risen's `far`) is drawn by proxies. Its cells still commit (they
  are the proxy's source and take deltas), but while covered they own no instance slots, no chunks
  and no occluders.
- **Covered** means the cell root has `render/DrawnByProxy`. Like `hidden`, `ComputedVisibility`
  inherits it as `proxied`. A proxied mesh gets no instance slot and its occluder is ignored. A
  proxied structure piece is left out of chunks, region meshes, shadow proxies and occluders, and
  its leaves aren't spawned. One write per cell, not per entity.
- **Order within the frame:** `hlod/select` (PostUpdate, after `TransformSystems`), then
  `render/compute-visibility`, then `structure/compile`, then render's prepare. Covering, uncovering
  and showing the proxy all land in one frame.
- **Rules per cell, each frame:**
  - at a non-proxy level: uncovered; its proxy, if any, is hidden;
  - at a proxy level, with a proxy built for its current content: covered, and the proxy draws;
  - at a proxy level with no proxy yet (first sight): uncovered, so it draws as a shell, as today,
    and is covered in the frame its proxy lands;
  - a proxy whose rebuild is pending keeps drawing.
- **One distance.** The swap is 0113's level swap (`full` range, with its hysteresis). Each full
  cell beyond `prebuild` × that range gets its proxy built ahead, from the full level, without
  `hlod/Exclude` subtrees (interiors). When the cell swaps to `far`, it's covered in the commit frame
  and structure never compiles the far level. Going back, the full level commits and the proxy
  hides in the same frame. Only interiors differ: proxies replace window panes with dark quads
  (`HlodSettings.windowColor`), so missing interiors never show.

### Members

- **Included:** what the cell draws, from its committed entities after dressing. That means
  structure pieces (as structure would build them), and every entity under the cell root with
  `Mesh3d` and `MeshMaterial`, whatever its blend mode.
- **Excluded:** subtrees under `hlod/Exclude`, window panes, skinned or morphed meshes, `Animator`,
  `DynamicCaster`, dynamic and kinematic bodies, `partition/Dynamic`, and `net/Replicated`
  entities.
- **Posing:** door leaves are merged at their current angle. A swinging door waits until it settles.
- **Settled:** a cell gathers only once every member's mesh and material is loaded and no member
  changed for `settleFrames`.
- **CPU data required:** a mesh with no CPU data is left out with `hlod/mesh-unreadable` (a warning).

### Build

1. **Gather** (main thread, `HlodSettings.gatherMs` per frame):
   - structure pieces as plain values;
   - each mesh member's attribute arrays (copied, or shared when the page is cross-origin
     isolated), world transform, material id and `NotShadowCaster`;
   - nearest cells first, `jobsInFlight` at most.
2. **Worker** (`worker/hlod`):
   - structure geometry, from `@aethervtt/shard-structure/geometry`: the builders compile uses,
     worker-safe, whole pieces unclipped;
   - mesh members transformed into world space, merged per material;
   - LODs, a positions-only shadow mesh, occluder triangles, bounds and a content digest.
3. **Commit** (main thread, `uploadBytes` per frame): virtual meshes (0042's `gen:` store, arena
   `'structure'`) and the proxy's entities. The old proxy despawns in the same frame its
   replacement shows.

- **LODs by part size, not surface simplification.** Box-built geometry has a seam at every hard
  edge, and meshoptimizer keeps 99.5–100% of it (measured). A part is a structure piece's mesh or a
  mesh member. Level k keeps the parts whose bounds diameter projects to at least `pixelError`
  pixels (1080 rows, the camera's field of view) at the distance where level k takes over. Up to
  `lodLevels` levels, each a mesh per material, switched by 0022's `Lod` with 0103's screen sizes.
  Ground and roofs never drop.
- **Proxy entities** (children of the partition root, `core/Derived`, never saved):
  - a root with `hlod/Proxy { grid, x, z, digest }`;
  - one `Mesh3d` + `Lod` child per material, with `NotShadowCaster` except for masked or
    double-sided materials, which cast as themselves;
  - one shadow-only child for the shadow mesh;
  - up to four `Occluder` children, one per 32 m quadrant, each capped at 256 triangles with the
    smallest dropped first.
- **Shadows.** The shadow mesh is LOD0's opaque, single-sided, standard-type triangles, in world
  space: the same triangles the cell's chunks give 0116's shadow proxies, less interiors. Proxies
  are static casters. A swap or rebuild is a rect redraw.
- **Occluders** come from proxies for covered cells, and from structure's chunks for uncovered
  cells. Coverage picks exactly one source per cell, each frame.

### Rebuilds

A covered or prebuilt cell is dirty when:
- a member's saved components change (change ticks: structure pieces, `Mesh3d`, `MeshMaterial`,
  `Transform`, `Visibility`);
- a member despawns;
- an entity with `Mesh3d` or a structure piece is added under its root.

Deltas (`applyCellDelta`), redressing and level swaps all arrive this way, so HLOD needs no hook per
source. Only that cell's proxy rebuilds. Its old proxy draws until the new one commits.

### File cells: offline proxies

A file cell (0072) can get its proxy from the cache instead of a runtime build.

- **`shard hlod build [partition]`** opens each stale cell headless, with the project's plugins
  but no game client systems.
  - It gathers members exactly as the runtime does, with prefab and scene instances expanded, and
    runs the same worker build.
  - Members are sorted by path and every job's split is fixed, so two builds give identical bytes.
  - A cell's record lives in `.shard/cache/hlod/<partition hash>/`: its meshes (0014's codec), its
    textures (KTX2), its bounds and its key.
  - The key is `sha256(the grid's hlod settings, the cell's member data (canonical), the artifact
    hashes of its meshes, materials and textures, the build version)`. A cell with no members
    contributes nothing, so an empty field costs no rebuild.
- **`shard dev`** rebuilds a cell's record in the background after the cell is saved, and the old
  proxy draws until then. **`shard export`** builds every record, and fails with `hlod/missing` if
  one can't be built. **`shard hlod status`** lists stale cells and why (which cell or asset
  changed).
- **At runtime** a file cell at a proxy level loads its record (nearest first, sharing 0072's
  `readsInFlight`) instead of gathering. If the cell has a runtime diff that touches a member, the
  runtime rebuilds it as `merged`, as for a generated cell: a baked record can't follow a change.
- **`baked`**, set per grid (`hlod: { method: 'baked', texelsPerMetre }`, file cells only):
  1. Merge, then simplify with meshoptimizer's attribute-free simplifier to the error at the swap
     distance. Seams don't matter, because the surface is re-textured.
  2. Unwrap with xatlas (WASM, loaded with `import()` by the build only).
  3. Render the members at full detail, with their own materials, into G-buffers from 14
     orthographic directions (the 6 axes and 8 corners). Any 0020 material, hooks included, writes
     the G-buffer as it does in a frame.
  4. For each atlas texel, blend the views that see it, each weighted by how directly it faces the
     normal.
  5. Write base colour, object-space normal and ORM, dilated by 4 texels. A texel no view sees
     takes the nearest seen value.
  - The proxy draws with an `hlod/Proxy` material, is lit dynamically, and casts like any other
    proxy.
  - A grid set to `baked` with generated cells logs `hlod/baked-generated` and builds `merged`.
  - With no GPU adapter, `baked` cells fail with `hlod/no-gpu` and the rest build. CI's software
    GPU is enough.

### Near cells: region meshes (structure)

With `StructureSettings.cameraRegions` (default true), camera views draw one merged mesh per
(32 m region, group, material) instead of each chunk mesh. That's 0116's shadow proxy with every
attribute. The ground group's 8 m floor chunks become 32 m regions.

- **Chunks:** they stay as the unit of rebuild. Their meshes are no longer instance slots, and
  their occluders stay per chunk.
- **Rebuilds:** an edit rebuilds its chunks, then their regions, in the same compile.
- **Door leaves** stay entities, and a door toggle still rebuilds nothing.

### API sketch

```ts
// @aethervtt/shard-hlod — client only, nothing registers on import
export function hlodPlugin(): Plugin
HlodSettings (resource) {
  enabled: bool = true, pixelError: f32 = 1.5, lodLevels: u8 = 3,
  prebuild: f32 = 0.85,          // × the first level's range
  jobsInFlight: u8 = 4, gatherMs: f32 = 0.5, uploadBytes: u32 = 4 << 20,
  settleFrames: u8 = 2, windowColor: vec3 = [0.02, 0.02, 0.025] }
Proxy { grid: u8, x: i32, z: i32, digest: u32 }   // hlod/Proxy, on each proxy root
export const Exclude: TagDef                      // hlod/Exclude: never in a proxy (interiors)
// partition: a level option, and per grid how file cells' proxies are built
levels: [{ name: 'full', range: 96 }, { name: 'far', range: 320, proxy: true }]
hlod: { method: 'merged' | 'baked' = 'merged', texelsPerMetre: f32 = 4 }   // baked: file cells only
// render
export const DrawnByProxy: TagDef                 // render/DrawnByProxy, inherited
ComputedVisibility { visible: bool, shadowOnly: bool, proxied: bool }
// structure
StructureSettings { ..., cameraRegions: bool = true }
export function structureGeometry(pieces: StructurePieces, options: GeometryOptions): StructureMeshes
  // @aethervtt/shard-structure/geometry: worker-safe, the builders compile uses
```

### What this asks of a game (Risen)

- **Interiors:** tag them `hlod/Exclude` in the full level: everything the far level drops (interior
  walls and floors, indoor props and finishes). Then a proxy prebuilt from `full` equals one built
  from `far`.
- **Exteriors:** the far level keeps everything visible from outside, as data: yard props' merged
  meshes, fences, porches, steps, and exterior `risen/Part` trim. Today `far` drops yard props, so
  the proxy would miss them.
- **Dressing:** none. `Mesh3d` stays on dressed entities, and coverage only drops their slots.
  Materials made at runtime are referenced, not copied.
- **Occlusion:** turn on `OcclusionCulling` on the camera once proxies land (today it slows the frame).
- **Breaking walls:** keep regenerating `risen/Part` meshes when walls change (Risen's ROADMAP step
  7). The proxy follows the `Dress` change.

### Agent surface

- `hlod.describe { ablate? }`:
  - per ring: cells, covered, prebuilt, building, stale, shown as shells;
  - proxy slots, triangles per LOD, bytes, jobs, and recent rebuilds with their cause;
  - with `ablate`, each ring hidden in turn for 60 frames, with its Δms, Δdraws and Δslots. That is
    the per-ring report.
- `hlod.cell { grid, x, z }`: the state, digest, member counts, and why it's stale.
- `hlod.preview { grid, x, z, distance? }`: the cell and its proxy rendered from the same viewpoint
  (by default the swap distance), both images, and their 0062 comparison.
- `render.capture { debug: 'hlod' }` tints proxies by LOD.
- `shard hlod build [partition] [--cells …]` and `shard hlod status`.
- MCP: `describe_hlod`, `preview_hlod`, `build_hlod`.
- Errors:
  - `hlod/mesh-unreadable` (a warning) and `hlod/baked-generated` (a warning);
  - `hlod/no-partition`, and `hlod/bad-level` (`proxy` on the first level);
  - `hlod/no-gpu`, `hlod/missing`;
  - `hlod/too-big`: an atlas over 4096² at its `texelsPerMetre`. The message suggests a lower
    density.

## Decisions

- **Rewritten in place, not superseded.** It was unbuilt, and five specs point at "HLOD (0073)".
- **Runtime proxies are `merged`; `baked` is offline only.**
  - `baked` at runtime would need xatlas and a texture encoder in the client. Shard has neither.
  - It would cost 14 G-buffer views per proxy on every rebuild.
  - Not popping at the 96 m swap needs about 10 texels/m (0.10 m per pixel at 1080p and 60°). For a
    far cell's ~4,850 m² that's a 1024² atlas: about 12 MB uncompressed per cell, about 1 GB for 80
    cells.
  - Offline, KTX2 compression and a coarser `texelsPerMetre` make sense, and nothing changes the
    cell while it's built.
  - `merged` builds in 1–7 ms on a worker (measured) and reuses the materials.
- **One build for both paths.** Offline and runtime proxies of the same content are the same
  bytes, so a file cell's runtime rebuild after a change matches what `shard hlod build` would
  write.
- **Projection baking through the G-buffer.** Every material, hooks included, bakes with no
  per-material code. Concave spots no view sees cost little at proxy distances.
- **Object-space normals in `baked`.** A remeshed, static proxy has no meaningful tangent frame.
- **No `instances` method.** Repeated members are door leaves of 12 triangles. Merging them costs
  less than a batch each.
- **Cover, don't hide.** Hidden slots cost 6.5 ms for 18.7k of them (measured). Covering removes
  slots, chunks and occluders.
- **Part-size LODs.** Structure geometry doesn't simplify (measured), and dropping parts under a
  pixel never moves a vertex.
- **One proxy per cell, no tree.** The far ring merges to about 800 slots at 320 m. A tree pays off
  past the loaded rings, which are out of scope.
- **Swap at the level swap, proxies prebuilt from `full`.** One distance. Far shells never compile
  once a proxy exists, and only interiors differ.
- **Dirty by change ticks, not hooks.** Deltas, dressing and swaps all touch members.
- **Occluders from whichever draws.** A covered cell's chunks don't exist.
- **Bytes are identical between builds, but proxies never enter a world hash.** Offline records
  must reproduce for caches and exports. Runtime proxies are only drawn, so a machine whose bytes
  differ costs nothing.
- **Region meshes are in this spec.** Without them the projected laptop frame (about 14 ms) has
  little margin, and occlusion only pays once batches are few.

## Acceptance criteria

Fixture: `test/DressedTownCell` (`partition/src/fixtures/dressed-town.ts`):
- 0116's street town with Risen's shape: houses dressed after commit by a client system (meshes
  and runtime materials), yard props, interiors tagged `hlod/Exclude`;
- 64 m cells, `full` to 96 m and `far` (proxy) to 320 m, giving about 84 far cells and 150 far
  houses at the start point;
- a sun with four on-change cascades, and occlusion on.

Budgets are in `bench/perf/budgets.json`, proposed.

- [ ] **Frame.** Walking the fixture at street level in third person, the `town-hlod` scenario
      meets its frame budget (CPU and GPU, laptop 16.7 / 16.6 ms, desktop 8.3), and
      `hlod.describe { ablate }` prints slots, draws and ms per ring. Risen's town meets the same
      budget in a `shard capture` plan on the laptop, recorded under As built.
- [ ] **Budgeted builds.** Over a 512 m walk:
      - main-thread HLOD time (`hlod/select`, `hlod/gather`, `hlod/commit`) meets `hlod/frame` at
        p95, and no frame exceeds `hlod/frame-max`;
      - worker builds meet `hlod/build` at p95;
      - no frame exceeds `partition/walk-frame` because of HLOD.
- [ ] **A broken wall.** The server removes a wall in a far cell (netHarness). The client's proxy
      shows the gap within `hlod/rebuild-latency`, exactly one proxy job runs, and no other proxy
      or chunk rebuilds (counts asserted). The old proxy draws on every frame until then.
- [ ] **Coverage.** Walking back and forth across the full/far boundary (±10 m) for 60 s, every
      cell in the rings draws exactly once on every frame:
      - checked logically: cell or proxy, never both or neither;
      - checked by holes against the sky colour.
- [ ] **Only interiors swap.** At each swap with a still camera:
      - 99.5% of the pixels that change lie inside the proxy's window quads (dilated 1 px);
      - the rest are within 0062's tolerance.
- [ ] **Shadows.** At the swap distance, the static cascade layers with the cell and with its proxy
      are bit-identical outside the house footprints. The frame's shadowed pixels outside windows
      differ in at most 0.1%.
- [ ] **Same content from either level.** A proxy prebuilt from `full` has the same digest as one
      built from `far`, for 20 cells with and without deltas.
- [ ] **Region meshes.** On 0116's street-town walk, structure's camera-view batches fall to
      `structure/region-batches` (0.3×) or below. Frames match per-chunk drawing within 0062's
      tolerance. A wall edit rebuilds its chunks and their regions only.
- [ ] **Occlusion pays.** With proxies and region meshes, the fixture's frame with occlusion is no
      slower than without.
- [ ] **Baseline.** The coverage, swap, shadow and rebuild tests pass with `SHARD_TIER=baseline` and
      on the fake GL context. The fixture walks on `?backend=webgl2` in the browser tests with
      coverage checked; its frame time is recorded, with no budget.
- [ ] **Headless.** `shard run` hashes are the same with `hlodPlugin` and without, and a server
      builds no proxy.

File cells, once 0072's importer loads them, on 0072's fixture (a town of 2,000 building entities):

- [ ] **Offline build.**
      - Editing one cell file rebuilds exactly that cell's record (count asserted).
      - Building the whole fixture meets `hlod/offline-build`.
      - Two builds give identical mesh bytes, and baked textures match within 0062's tolerance
        across Dawn on Metal and WARP.
      - A file cell's runtime `merged` rebuild matches its offline record byte for byte.
- [ ] **Baked quality.** Across 20 cells, `hlod.preview` at the swap distance is within 0062's
      tolerance. A material with a custom fragment hook bakes to the colour it renders in a frame.
      Proxy texture memory stays under `hlod/texture-memory`.
- [ ] **No GPU.** With no adapter, `baked` cells fail with `hlod/no-gpu` and the `merged` ones
      build. `shard export` fails with `hlod/missing` while a record is missing.

## Open questions

- **Past the last ring.** Proxies kept for unloaded cells would show a horizon, but the server sends
  no deltas there, so they'd go stale. Deferred: a game asking for view distances past ~500 m.
- **The GPU frame is unmeasured.** Proposed: ablate the passes on the laptop before marking this
  built.
- **One draw per proxy.** A per-vertex material index (0055's open question) if the laptop still
  misses its budget. Proposed: after measuring.
- **Coarser layers for file cells** (0073's old tree of 4 × 4 and 16 × 16 blocks). These only pay
  off past the loaded rings, so they follow the first question.
